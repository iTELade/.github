## About iTELade

iTELade focuses on **self-hosted infrastructure, IT operations, service management and security-oriented tooling**.

We build and maintain practical systems for day-to-day administration: identity and access management, networking, DNS, mail, monitoring, automation and internal service platforms.

## Operations

Public-facing service availability is published at **[status.itelade.pl](https://status.itelade.pl)**.

Administrative services are kept separate from public-facing applications, with access controls built around private networking, centralized identity and least-privilege principles.

## Open source

Public repositories are used for projects that can be developed openly. Internal infrastructure configuration, credentials and private operational data remain outside public repositories.
